# Digital Product Research

This project is a lightweight research stage for finding and evaluating digital products that solve specific, recurring problems for identifiable customers. It focuses on evidence of existing demand and spending, products that can be delivered repeatedly with limited fulfillment and maintenance, and realistic distribution opportunities. It does not promise passive income or guaranteed revenue.

## Run the researcher

In Claude Code, ask the `product-researcher` subagent to investigate a specific opportunity. For example:

> Use the product-researcher subagent to research digital products for independent wedding photographers who struggle to follow up with leads.

Give it a customer or problem to investigate; it will research the market and present concepts without choosing one for you.

## Output

The concise, sourced opportunity report is saved to `research/opportunity-brief.md`.

## Approval before building

This project currently covers research only. Human approval is required before moving on to product creation. The researcher must not build or publish a product, make purchases, or modify external accounts.
