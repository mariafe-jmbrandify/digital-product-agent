# Technician / Vendor Handoff

*Validation prototype — confirm property policy and authorization before dispatch.*

## Before dispatch

- [ ] Confirm ticket ID, property, unit, reported issue, and exact location.
- [ ] Confirm priority and response expectation under property policy.
- [ ] Share relevant resident observations and troubleshooting already completed.
- [ ] Confirm approved access method, appointment window, contact, and any access restrictions.
- [ ] Share known site conditions (parking, entry, pets, occupied/vacant) as appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm scope requested; do not imply approval for extra work.
- [ ] Request estimate and written approval before work when required by policy.
- [ ] Confirm who updates the resident and property manager, and by when.

## After dispatch

- [ ] Confirm appointment and access with the designated contact.
- [ ] Record arrival, findings, work performed, and any parts/materials used.
- [ ] Contact the authorized approver before out-of-scope or additional-cost work.
- [ ] Report incomplete work, parts delays, return visit, or newly identified issue.
- [ ] Send completion notes and photos/documentation through the approved channel.
- [ ] Identify whether a follow-up visit, inspection, or resident confirmation is needed.
