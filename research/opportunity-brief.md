# Digital Product Opportunity Research

## 1. Opportunity
**OBSERVATION:** Property, field-service, CRM, support, and delivery software already packages recurring workflows as paid features. This supports researching complementary, implementation-ready playbooks—not a claim that standalone templates sell. **HYPOTHESIS:** narrowly scoped digital kits could reduce setup effort for small operators who do not want a custom consultant or a new platform.

## 2. Target Customer
**HYPOTHESES:** independent property managers; small trade contractors; GoHighLevel (GHL) agencies serving those niches; small operations teams introducing AI; and local delivery businesses. Start with one operator type and one workflow, rather than a generic “operations bundle.”

## 3. Problem
**HYPOTHESIS:** these buyers may need repeatable ways to intake, prioritize, hand off, document, and close operational work. Existing platforms offer features, but configuration, staff adoption, exception handling, and written procedures remain potential gaps. The specific gap and willingness to pay require customer interviews.

## 4. Evidence of Demand
**FACT:** TenantCloud describes plans spanning basic maintenance to automation, reporting, team features, and integrations [S1]; it also offers a contractor-discovery route for maintenance [S2]. Jobber sells field-service plans and lists workflow automation, job costing, scheduling, and related functions [S3, S4]. Intercom prices its AI support product per seat and per outcome [S5]. **OBSERVATION:** these are evidence of established paid software categories and workflow priorities—not proof of demand for independent template products. No reliable sales volume for comparable digital downloads was verified.

## 5. Existing Products
**FACT:** TenantCloud and property-management platforms address rental operations; Jobber and Housecall Pro offer field-service software [S1, S3, S6]. GHL markets CRM/automation tools and has an app marketplace [S7, S8]. Intercom sells customer-support software with AI-agent features [S5]. Onfleet is an established delivery-operations product [S9]. These are adjacent or direct software competitors; none was verified as an exact substitute for each proposed toolkit.

## 6. Competitor Pricing
**FACT:** Jobber lists plans from $29 to $529/month (annual-billing presentation; user/team configuration changes prices) [S3]. Intercom lists $29/seat/month for Essential, with Fin AI at $0.99 per outcome; higher tiers cost more [S5]. **OBSERVATION:** these recurring software prices are only context, not comparable downloadable-template prices. Numeric prices were not available in the TenantCloud or Onfleet page captures; GHL’s page warns usage-based charges may apply [S1, S7]. No verified digital-download price benchmark is included.

## 7. Product Formats
1. **Property maintenance desk kit:** editable request form, triage matrix, vendor quote/approval log, resident updates, escalation scripts, and closeout checklist (Docs/Sheets/Notion).
2. **Trade job-closeout kit:** job photo checklist, change-order and customer sign-off forms, callback/root-cause log, invoice handoff, and role-based SOP (Docs/Sheets/PDF).
3. **GHL niche workflow pack:** importable workflows, pipeline stages, field map, message templates, setup checklist, and test cases for one niche (GHL snapshot plus guide).
4. **AI support operations playbook:** knowledge-base template, response-quality rubric, escalation rules, approved prompt examples, and review log (Docs/Sheets).
5. **Local delivery exception kit:** late/failed-delivery decision tree, customer notification scripts, proof-of-delivery checklist, incident log, and dispatcher handoff (Docs/Sheets).

## 8. Low-Maintenance Potential
**OBSERVATION:** editable files can be delivered automatically through a storefront; fulfillment is low-touch if setup instructions and examples are complete. Templates need periodic link, compatibility, and regulatory review; customization and integrations add support burden. GHL workflows are highest-maintenance because account settings, platform behavior, and client processes vary. Keep updates versioned, define support limits, and sell customization separately if tested. No product has been built or tested.

## 9. Distribution Opportunities
**HYPOTHESES:** search-led guides and downloadable checklists; property-management and contractor newsletters/communities; co-marketing with bookkeeping or implementation consultants; GHL agency partnerships and its app ecosystem; and direct outreach to local operators. Validate channel access and acquisition cost before relying on any channel. GHL’s app marketplace is confirmed, but it is not evidence of a snapshot sales channel [S8].

## 10. Differentiation Opportunities
**HYPOTHESES:** focus on one operational “handoff” with realistic edge cases; include usable blank and completed examples; map the kit to existing tools instead of requiring migration; provide role-specific variants and a quick-start test plan; and make implementation boundaries explicit. Test usability with actual operators before claiming time savings or compliance.

## 11. Risks
**HYPOTHESES:** free templates and bundled software features may substitute; buyers may prefer implementation help; low standalone prices may not cover marketing/support; platform changes can break GHL assets; AI or customer messaging may create privacy, accuracy, or escalation risks; and procedures may need jurisdiction-specific legal or safety review. Software pricing and feature pages do not resolve these risks.

## 12. Validation Tests
Interview 5–8 people in each narrowly selected customer segment about their last real workflow failure and current workaround. Show a sample artifact, request a paid preorder or refundable pilot commitment before building the full kit, and track objections. For GHL, test import/setup in several clean subaccounts. For AI, run human-reviewed scenarios and document failure/escalation paths. Treat interest, compliments, and survey intent as weaker evidence than actual payment or repeated use.

## 13. Product Concepts
- **Maintenance Request-to-Resolution Kit** — independent property managers; inconsistent intake/vendor/resident follow-up; editable SOP + tracker bundle; **$29–79 (HYPOTHESIS)**; might buy to standardize handoffs without replacing their property platform; validate recurring requests and willingness to use a separate tracker.
- **Small-Trade Job Closeout & Callback Kit** — owner-operators and office managers; missing photos, sign-offs, or callback learning; printable/mobile-friendly forms + SOP; **$29–99 (HYPOTHESIS)**; might buy to make job completion consistent; validate fit across trades and current software workflows.
- **GHL Property-Service Workflow Snapshot** — GHL agencies/implementers; repeat configuration for one service niche; snapshot + field map + QA checklist; **$49–199 (HYPOTHESIS)**; might buy to reduce repeat setup; validate snapshot compatibility, agency interest, and platform update burden.
- **Human-Supervised AI Support SOP Pack** — small customer-support teams; inconsistent AI answers/escalations; response rubric + knowledge-base and review templates; **$39–129 (HYPOTHESIS)**; might buy a practical governance starting point; validate whether buyers need guidance beyond vendor documentation.
- **Local Delivery Exception Desk Kit** — small fleets/local delivery operators; inconsistent handling of delays, failed drops, and proof; dispatch scripts + incident tracker; **$29–99 (HYPOTHESIS)**; might buy to standardize exceptions without buying another dispatch tool; validate operator access and whether existing systems already cover the workflow.

No winner selected. Price ranges above are unvalidated hypotheses, not revenue forecasts.
