# Recruitment Form — Maintenance Workflow Research

*Google Form. Purpose: determine research fit and schedule a 15-minute interview. Collect nothing else. Hidden field: `source` (from link tag).*

**Form title:** Maintenance Request Workflow — Research Interview

**Form description:**
> I'm researching how property managers handle maintenance requests from intake to resolution. This is research only — nothing is being sold. If there's a fit, I'll email you a link to book a 15-minute call. Please don't include any tenant, resident, or vendor details. Takes about 2 minutes.

## Questions

1. **What is your role in handling maintenance requests?** *(required, multiple choice)*
   - I manage or coordinate maintenance requests myself
   - I oversee someone who coordinates them
   - I'm occasionally involved
   - I'm not involved in maintenance requests

2. **What type of property do you manage?** *(required, checkboxes)*
   - Residential rental — single-family
   - Residential rental — multifamily
   - HOA / condo association
   - Commercial
   - Other: ___

3. **Which best describes your organization?** *(required, multiple choice)*
   - Self-managing owner / landlord
   - Independent or small property management company
   - Larger property management company
   - Other: ___

4. **Roughly how many units do you manage?** *(required, multiple choice)*
   - 1–10 · 11–50 · 51–200 · 201–500 · More than 500

5. **When did you last handle or oversee a maintenance request?** *(required, multiple choice)*
   - In the last 30 days
   - 1–6 months ago
   - More than 6 months ago

6. **What do you currently use to track maintenance requests?** *(optional, checkboxes)*
   - Property-management software (name, if you like): ___
   - Spreadsheet
   - Email / text messages
   - Paper or notebook
   - Other: ___

7. **First name** *(required, short answer)*

8. **Email address for scheduling** *(required, short answer, email validation)*

9. **Time zone** *(required, dropdown)*

10. **Consent** *(required, checkbox)*
    - ☐ I agree to be contacted by email about a 15-minute research interview. I understand this is not a sales call, participation is voluntary, I can withdraw at any time, and I can ask for my details to be deleted.

**Confirmation message:**
> Thanks! If there's a fit, you'll get an email with a link to book a time. If not, I may not follow up — thank you for taking the time.

## Deliberately not collected

Phone number, company name, property addresses, tenant/resident or vendor information, budget, pricing opinions, or purchase intent. Those topics are handled (or excluded) in the interview itself.
